How to read the map

Each glowing point is one NOAA report (bigger = significant or damaging). Pillars with pulsing rings mark the most damaging events in the filters.

Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. Hover a column for values; click it to select that map cell.

NOAA damage values are estimates entered by the National Weather Service, not insured losses. Many are blank or rough.
